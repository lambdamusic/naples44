Contessa della Peruta

A beautiful, refined aristocrat living in a near-castle above Santa Maria della Colombina.

In the book

The subject of an ‘extraordinary’ marriage vetting: the elegance turns out to be staged with borrowed furniture in an otherwise empty house — a sharp variation on the genteel poverty Lewis keeps meeting.
