Person · Allied colleague

Neil Armstrong

A Field Security colleague Lewis last saw in Tunisia eighteen months before.

In the book

Their September 1944 reunion in Benevento — Armstrong now so wary that Lewis half-expects him to grope for his testicles at the sight of a stranger — becomes the book’s measure of how completely the south changes a man.
