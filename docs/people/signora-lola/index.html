Signora Lola

A war widow, once the mistress of a Fascist hierarch, now Captain Frazer’s lover.

In the book

Asks Lewis to interpret her affair with the wordless Frazer and to settle its ‘basic matters’; over the following months he follows the relationship through the Capri trip, the break-ups and suicide threats, and her retirement to Ischia ‘to deflate’ once Frazer is posted away.
