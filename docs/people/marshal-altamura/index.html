Person · Police official

Marshal Altamura

The SIM secret-police officer who effectively runs Benevento — ‘handsome, good-humoured, quite imperturbable’ and exuding ‘sinister power’.

In the book

Ordered by Naples to place himself at Lewis’s disposal, he stage-manages Lewis’s introduction to the town’s notables; but an anonymous letter, and the wounding of Durham, leave Lewis increasingly uneasy that the Marshal is in league with the bandits.

Appears in 2 diary entries.

    The local Marshal frets over bandit threats to the town, but Lewis notes how absurdly under-armed and unprepared the local security forces really are.
