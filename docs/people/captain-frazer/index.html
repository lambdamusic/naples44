Captain Frazer

A tall, handsome RASC captain whose hands are on military supplies.

In the book

Lola’s lover; Lewis is roped in to interpret for the couple and becomes a bemused witness to the whole affair — the white-bread courtship, Frazer’s health wrecked by Lola’s demands, the golden Capri outing, the break-ups and reconciliations, and finally his posting away.
