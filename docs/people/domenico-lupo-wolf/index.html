Person · Camorrista / bandit

Domenico 'Lupo' (Wolf)

A young, handsome, dashing bandit of Frattamaggiore — robber of the rich and giver to the poor.

In the book

Freed from prison by the advancing Allies, he rebuilds his band with a forged AMG travel pass; Lewis watches, as an onlooker, the police operation that finally takes him, sprung by his jealous mistress.
