Albano

The partisan leader of Torrito, a village near Aversa.

In the book

Lewis reluctantly travels to Torrito to hear his account of the ‘heroic four days’ of the September 1943 uprising and the SS reprisal that followed — a massacre that has left the whole village in mourning. He finds Albano a haggard, haunted man who speaks very quietly, as if afraid of being overheard.
