Captain Cartwright

The section’s Field Security Officer.

In the book

Smashed up in a car crash the day before embarkation and left in hospital in Oran, so the section lands in Italy leaderless; when he finally reaches them, his face ‘covered with plaster’, it is to admit that their presence at Paestum serves ‘no purpose of any kind’.
