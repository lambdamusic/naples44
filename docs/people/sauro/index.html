Person · Notable civilian

Sauro

A young soldier — Italian father, German mother — behind the October 1943 delayed-mine hoax that emptied Naples.

In the book

Sent to decide whether he should be shot as a spy, Lewis finds not a ‘gigantic pale-eyed Teuton’ but a small, hungry boy, and reports him mentally unbalanced to save his life.
