Person · Notable civilian

Giuseppina

A ‘begrimed and bedraggled’ twelve-year-old orphan in Benevento who lives ‘under a house’ by the river.

In the book

The first abandoned girl Lewis has seen among the city’s hundreds of boy-orphans; she comes matter-of-factly to the office to collect her weekly blanket — a currency worth a factory-worker’s wage.
