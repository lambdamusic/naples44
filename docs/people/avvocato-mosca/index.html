Person · Informant / contact

Avvocato Mosca

An impoverished Naples lawyer, entitled to style himself Conte but no longer bothering.

In the book

One of the trio of genteel-poor bachelor contacts — with Losurdo and Lattarullo — whose grand, mouldering, half-empty palazzi on the Via Chiaia become Lewis’s picture of the ruined Neapolitan gentry.
