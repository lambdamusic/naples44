Person · Camorrista / bandit

Fortuna

A well-connected penicillin racketeer, a lieutenant of Vito Genovese.

In the book

Lewis visits him in Poggio Reale and finds him serenely certain of acquittal despite the evidence — and he is proved right: an emblem of how the powerful stay ‘beyond the reach of the law’ while petty thieves rot in the cells.
