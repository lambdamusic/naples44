Eric Williams

The section’s best Italian speaker.

In the book

Sent as ‘a solitary exile’ to run security in Nola, where the Feast of the Lily brings the town — and the army’s telephone lines — to a standstill; his complaints supply one of the book’s set-piece feast stories.
