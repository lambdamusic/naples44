Donna Maria Fidora ('La Pitonessa')

The richest landowner near Caivano, a former circus performer who had wrestled a python — hence ‘La Pitonessa’.

In the book

Lewis’s first contact inside the Camorra zone outside the police; barred as a woman from the Camorra itself but a valuable informant, she runs her late husband’s estate with professional efficiency and turns up, incongruously, at Lewis’s hospital bedside.
