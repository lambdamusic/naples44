Person · Allied colleague

Sergeant-Major (John) Dashwood

The section’s warrant officer.

In the book

In the confusion after the landing, sends four of the section by motorcycle along the shore to Salerno in the hope of finding the Field Security Officer and an order releasing them from their ‘absurd predicament’. Runs the section through those leaderless first weeks, and is later the bearer of unwelcome HQ news — including the cut to informant funds.
