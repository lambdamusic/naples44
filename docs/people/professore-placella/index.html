Professore Placella

A Naples gynaecologist whose speciality is the surgical restoration of virginity — his replacement, he boasts, is better than the original.

In the book

Parkinson’s most valuable medical contact; he helps expose the hospital racket by which syphilitic prostitutes bought clean bills of health, and later helps spring the trap on the coercive VD round-up.

Appears in 2 diary entries.

    A Neapolitan prince and his sister calmly request that she be admitted to a (nonexistent) army brothel, illustrating the era's blunt attitudes to sex and survival.
