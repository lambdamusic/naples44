Person · Informant / contact

Vincente Lattarullo

One of Naples’s four thousand briefless lawyers — ‘distinguished, fragile-looking’ and slowly starving; ‘motivated’, he says, ‘by a passion for justice’.

In the book

Lewis’s ‘prize acquisition’ among his civilian contacts and, more than that, his window onto the city: the borrowed-furniture lunch, the sideline as a hired ‘uncle from Rome’ at funerals, and his complete ignorance of the vendetta country ten miles from his café all become set-pieces of the book.
