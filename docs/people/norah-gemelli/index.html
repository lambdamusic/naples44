Person · Civilian friend

Norah Gemelli

A Naples neighbour of the arrested Signora Esposito-Lau, with an Irish mother and perfect English; she reads Dante and Leopardi aloud.

In the book

The centre of the circle of friends Lewis makes and treasures — and, at a dinner, the source of the true Anzio-landing leak he is then ordered to chase down, at the cost of their trust.
