Saint / folklore

flying monk of Pomigliano

A stigmatic monk of Pomigliano credited with the power of flight — identified in the book’s own footnote as the future Padre Pio.

In the book

He claims to have flown through the air to rescue a downed pilot, and displays the stigmata — one of the wave of miracles sweeping the region in 1944.
