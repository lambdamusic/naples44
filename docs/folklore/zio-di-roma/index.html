Saint / folklore

Zio di Roma

A hired mourner who impersonates a distinguished ‘uncle from Rome’ at a funeral to raise the bereaved family’s standing.

In the book

Lattarullo’s suspended sideline, resumed once Rome falls — complete with a rented car and driver and a special ‘Zio di Roma’ suit.
