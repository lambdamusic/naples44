Agropoli

Agropoli is a town and comune located in the Cilento area of the province of Salerno, Campania, Italy. It is situated at the start of the Cilentan Coast, on the Tyrrhenian Sea.

In the book

A coastal town south of Salerno cited in the newspapers’ gleeful catalogue of audacious wartime thefts — here, small ships lifted bodily out of the water and spirited away.

Appears in 1 diary entry.
