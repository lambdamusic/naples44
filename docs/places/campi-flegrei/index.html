Campi Flegrei

The Phlegraean Fields is a large volcanic caldera west of Naples, Italy. The Neapolitan Yellow Tuff eruption produced just 50 cubic kilometers. It is, however, one of relatively few volcanoes large enough to form a caldera.

In the book

The volcanic area west of Naples where crowds gather in spring 1944 around a twelve-year-old visionary — a ‘local Bernadette’ — to whom the Virgin is said to appear.

Appears in 1 diary entry.

  1. March 29 1944
    More reported miracles sweep the region, including a visionary child and a monk claimed to have flown to rescue a downed pilot.
