Capri

Capri is an island located in the Tyrrhenian Sea off the Sorrento Peninsula, on the south side of the Gulf of Naples in the Campania region of Italy. A resort destination since the time of the Roman Republic, it is known for its natural beauty, historic sites, and tourism industry. It has 13,451 inhabitants.

In the book

The island escape — a golden, carefree boat trip with Captain Frazer and Lola stands out sharply against the war, and the island reappears through Frazer’s turbulent romance.
