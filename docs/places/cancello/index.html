Cancello

In the book

A village near which five Moroccan soldiers are lured into a house and poisoned in revenge for atrocities; also the field hospital where the captured saboteur Salvatore Loreto turns up.

Appears in 2 diary entries.

  1. June 4 1944
    A brutal act of vigilante revenge against Moroccan soldiers accused of atrocities is reported, carried out with ritualized, disturbing cruelty.
    • War & violence
    • Crime & banditry
  2. August 3 1944
    Lewis assesses the security risk of holding a wounded, notorious Italian saboteur in a hospital ward run by a nurse he knew previously in Algeria.
