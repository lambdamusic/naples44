Ischia

Ischia is a volcanic island in the Tyrrhenian Sea. It lies at the northern end of the Gulf of Naples, about 30 kilometres from the city of Naples. It is the largest of the Phlegrean Islands.

In the book

The Bay island where Frazer’s mistress Lola and her friend retire for the summer once the affair ends; also noted for the deep ashfall it takes during the eruption.
