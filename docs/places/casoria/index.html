Casoria

Casoria is a city and comune (municipality) in the Metropolitan City of Naples in the region of Campania in southern Italy, located about 5 kilometres northeast of Naples. It has 73,257 inhabitants.

In the book

Another of the poor towns north of Naples that Lewis polices, tied to the fruitless wire-theft investigation and the wider Camorra-country cases.
