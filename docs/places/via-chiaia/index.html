Once the resort of the town’s aristocracy, now lined with vast dark half-empty palazzi; Lewis’s impoverished-noble contacts Losurdo and Mosca live here, and a marriage candidate’s flat nearby gives her away by the fortune in black-market soap in the kitchen.
