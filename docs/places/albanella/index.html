Albanella

Albanella is a town and municipality in the province of Salerno in the Campania region of south-western Italy. It is 51 kilometres (32 mi) from the city of Salerno.

In the book

The rail station just south of the Sele where Fifth Army HQ — and the parasitically attached section — sits during the battle for the beachhead; German tanks push toward it to within a mile of Lewis’s slit trench.

Appears in 2 diary entries.
