Place · Natural feature

Calore river

In the book

One of the two rivers, with the Sele, bounding the tongue of land down which German tanks advanced toward Albanella during the battle for the Salerno beachhead.

Appears in 1 diary entry.

  1. September 14 1943
    An intense night battle near the beachhead, with naval bombardment lighting the sky and hurried, ad hoc arming of the intelligence men.
