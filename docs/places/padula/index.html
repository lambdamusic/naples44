Padula

Padula is a town and comune in the province of Salerno, located in the Campania region of south-western Italy. The town is best known for the Carthusian monastery of Certosa di San Lorenzo, it is one of the largest monasteries in Italy and a notable example of Baroque architecture. The Certosa is included in the UNESCO World Heritage Site.

In the book

The former monastery used as an internment camp: the writer Curzio Malaparte has been quietly released from it, and it is where the section must collect a female internee too important to be named in writing.
