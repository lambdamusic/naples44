Acerra

Acerra is a city and comune (municipality) in the Metropolitan City of Naples in the region of Campania, southern Italy, about 15 kilometres northeast of the capital in Naples. It has 58,585 inhabitants.

In the book

One of the ring of towns north of Naples put under Lewis’s security charge (with Casoria, Afragola and Aversa), all of them, he discovers, inside Camorra territory. He calls on its skeleton Carabinieri force and vets a local girl hoping to marry a British guardsman.
