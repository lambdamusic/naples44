Afragola

Afragola is a city and comune (municipality) in the Metropolitan City of Naples in the region of Campania in southern Italy. It has 61,581 inhabitants.

In the book

One of the small towns ringing Naples that Lewis polices, standing in for the poor, Camorra-shadowed hinterland just beyond the city. It recurs around denunciations, vendettas, wire-theft prosecutions and the bandit Domenico ‘Lupo’.
