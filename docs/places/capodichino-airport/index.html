Capodichino airport

Naples-Capodichino International Airport is the international airport serving Naples, its metropolitan city and the Campania region. Located 3.2 NM north-northeast of the city in Naples, within the Capodichino area of the San Pietro a Patierno district, the airport is officially named Aeroporto di Napoli-Capodichino Ugo Niutta, after decorated WWI pilot Ugo Niutta.

In the book

Naples’s airport; Lewis goes there to collect an Italian general said to have been drugged and kidnapped — one of the ‘Maugham-ish’ episodes of his intelligence work.
