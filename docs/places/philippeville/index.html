Philippeville

Skikda is a city in northeastern Algeria and a port on the Mediterranean. It is the capital of Skikda Province and Skikda District.

In the book

The Algerian town whose notorious prison pit — where dissidents were flung into total darkness — is the only jail Lewis has known before he is sent into Poggio Reale.
