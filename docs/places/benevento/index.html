Benevento

Benevento is a city and comune (municipality) of Campania, Italy, capital of the province of Benevento. It is situated on a hill 130 metres above sea level at the confluence of the Calore Irpino and the Sabato. As of June 30, 2025, Benevento has 55,319 inhabitants.

In the book

Where Lewis is posted, alone, in August 1944 to run security for a city wrecked by an air raid and sunk in near-universal mourning. His weeks here are dominated by typhus and typhoid, the arrival of undisciplined Canadian troops, and an escalating, dangerous campaign against motorised bandits.
