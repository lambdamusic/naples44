San Giorgio (near Vesuvius)

San Giorgio a Cremano is a city and comune (municipality) in the Metropolitan City of Naples in the region of Campania in southern Italy. It is located on the foothills of Mount Vesuvius to the west of the volcano and the Tyrrhenian Sea, and is five kilometres to the south east of the centre of Naples. The municipality has views of Mount Vesuvius, Mount Somma and the Bay of Naples.
