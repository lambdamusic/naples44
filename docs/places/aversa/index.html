Aversa

Aversa is a city and comune in the Province of Caserta in Campania, southern Italy, about 24 km north of Naples. It is the centre of an agricultural district, the Agro Aversano, producing wine and cheese. Aversa is also the main seat of the faculties of Architecture and Engineering of the Università degli studi della Campania “L.

In the book

One of the towns north of Naples in Lewis’s patch, recurring with famine-driven crime, impoverished nobility and village denunciations.
