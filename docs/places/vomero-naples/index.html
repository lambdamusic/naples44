Vomero (Naples)

Vomero is a hilltop quarter of Naples, Italy — comprising approximately two square kilometres (0.77 sq mi) and a population of 48,000.

In the book

The hilltop quarter, associated with the section’s informant network and with several intelligence flaps — the false-mine mass evacuation, the traced Anzio leak.
