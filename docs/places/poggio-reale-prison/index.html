Place · Landmark

Poggio Reale prison

In the book

The prison and its courts, where Lewis’s cases keep ending up — a German held over the mine hoax, the well-connected racketeer Fortuna expecting acquittal, petty thieves tried within sight of an open black market. It embodies the book’s sense of justice falling only on the powerless.
