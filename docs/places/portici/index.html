Portici

Portici is a city and comune (municipality) of the Metropolitan City of Naples in the region of Campania in southern Italy. It has 51,213 inhabitants.

In the book

One of the grimy industrial towns fused into a twelve-mile suburb along the coast south of Naples, and — with Torre del Greco — an area especially exposed to Vesuvius, with fears for its military installations during the 1944 eruption.

Appears in 1 diary entry.

  1. October 4 1943
    Discharged and hitching toward newly-liberated Naples, Lewis passes through Battipaglia, devastated by Allied carpet bombing, and learns nearby Altavilla was shelled to rubble on the mere suspicion it held Germans. Further on, he witnesses desperate Neapolitan housewives publicly exchanging sex for tins of rations with American soldiers.
