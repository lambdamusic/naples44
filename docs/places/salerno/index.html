Place · Town

Salerno

Salerno is a city and comune in Campania, southern Italy, and the capital of the province of the same name. With 125,958 inhabitants as of 2025, it is the second most populous municipality in the region after Naples. The city lies on the Gulf of Salerno, on the Tyrrhenian Sea.

In the book

The invasion beachhead where the book opens. Lewis lands nearby in September 1943 into chaos — a German counter-attack, gas scares, rumours the beachhead will be abandoned — and the town stays a reference point for the campaign’s uncertain first weeks.
