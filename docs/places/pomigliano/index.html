Pomigliano

Pomigliano d’Arco is a municipality in the Metropolitan City of Naples in Italy, located north of Mount Vesuvius.

In the book

The town of the ‘flying monk’, a friar who claims to have flown to rescue a downed pilot and displays the stigmata — one of the wave of wartime miracles Lewis catalogues in spring 1944.

Appears in 1 diary entry.

  1. March 29 1944
    More reported miracles sweep the region, including a visionary child and a monk claimed to have flown to rescue a downed pilot.
