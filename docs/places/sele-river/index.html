Sele river

In the book

The river by the Salerno beachhead, where HQ sits through the tense, underdefended first days and the night battles of September 1943.

Appears in 3 diary entries.

  1. September 11 1943
    HQ moves to a tranquil rural setting, but there's a creeping sense that the Fifth Army is unprepared and undefended.

    An account of a botched American paratroop drop, followed by the grim sight of German tank crews killed in their vehicles.
