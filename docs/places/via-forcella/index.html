Via Forcella

In the book

The street through the Forcella market, recurring with the wire-theft case and the open trade in stolen Allied goods.

Appears in 3 diary entries.

  1. January 1 1944
    A tedious, unproductive investigation into telephone-wire theft, which the Army wrongly treats as sabotage rather than opportunistic copper theft.
    • Black market
    • Bureaucracy & absurdity
  2. May 9 1944
    The black market's scale and brazenness are staggering — stolen Allied goods are openly, even elegantly, advertised for sale near the courts trying petty thieves.
    • Black market
  3. June 7 1944
    Lattarullo's improvised income as a professional "mourning relative" at funerals picks up again with the fall of Rome, complete with a rented car and driver.
    • Naples's character
