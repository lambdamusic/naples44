May 7 (ii) 1944

Frustrated by unresolved banditry, the General pressures Italian police into a harsher new anti-bandit operation, which Lewis observes as an onlooker.

Themes

  • Crime & banditry
  • Bureaucracy & absurdity

Reflections

Injustice of the anti-bandit crackdown

Lewis opens by calling the General's mass-arrest campaign against bandits 'a shameful example of the perfidiousness and injustices of this war we conduct behind the scenes' — the crackdown falls only on small-time opportunists, not real gang leadership.

Places

  • Frattamaggiore Town
  • Poggio Reale prison Landmark
  • Procida Town

People

  • Domenico 'Lupo' (Wolf) Camorrista / bandit captured after a police operation exploiting his jealous mistress; his downfall observed by Lewis as an onlooker
