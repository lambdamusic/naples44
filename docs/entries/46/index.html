February 28 1944

The arrival of spring in Naples is marked by seasonal foods, street cries, and a burst of communal spring-cleaning and song.

Themes

  • Naples's character

Reflections

Historical amnesia of the ordinary Neapolitan

Watching street storytellers recount the deeds of Charlemagne, Lewis reflects that ten centuries of invading armies and drowned revolutions have left no mark on the common Neapolitan's imagination or memory.
