June 27 1944

A colleague describes a revived, medieval-style flagellant procession at Guardia Sanframondi; two more feast stories follow — Nola's Feast of the Lily disrupting army telephone lines, and a trip to Amalfi for the thrice-yearly 'manna' relic of St Andrew.

People

  • Eric Williams Allied colleague describes his frustrations with the Feast of the Lily disrupting telephone lines at Nola

Saints & folklore

  • Guardia Sanframondi flagellant rite flagellant procession held every seven years in honour of the Virgin
  • Festival of the Lilies (Nola) Feast of St Paulinus; giant wooden 'lily' towers paraded through Nola, cutting army telephone cables in the process
  • St Andrew's Manna (Amalfi) relic in Amalfi Cathedral's crypt said to exude a miraculous liquid several times a year; Del Giudice queues to buy a sample
