October 24 1944

Lewis is abruptly ordered to a new mission repatriating Russian ex-POWs, sensing this marks the end of his time in Naples.

Themes

  • Bureaucracy & absurdity
  • Personal reflection

Reflections

Abrupt departure — no time for goodbyes

Suddenly ordered to leave for a new posting, Lewis reflects with wistful regret on all the small rituals of Naples life he will have no time to close out — one last marsala with a contact, one last view from the Vomero, one last meal at Zi' Teresa's.
