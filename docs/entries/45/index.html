February 12 1944

Lewis reflects on the cultural gulf between cosmopolitan Naples and the archaic, vendetta-bound countryside just miles away, as seen through Lattarullo's ignorance of it.

Themes

  • Naples's character
  • Personal reflection

Reflections

Naples's cultural distance from its own hinterland

Lewis observes that cosmopolitan Naples knows and cares nothing for the archaic, vendetta-bound countryside just miles away — his contact Lattarullo has visited Rome many times but never once turned aside into nearby Afragola.
