October 24 1943

Lewis is sent to investigate whether the German soldier behind the mine hoax should be executed as a spy.

Themes

  • War & violence
  • Bureaucracy & absurdity

Reflections

Generals passing moral responsibility

Asked to help decide whether to execute a captured teenage German soldier, Lewis observes that generals who like to appear ruthless are often eager in practice to offload the moral responsibility for such decisions onto subordinates like himself.

Places

  • Philippeville Town
  • Poggio Reale prison Landmark

People

  • Sauro Notable civilian young Italian-German soldier behind the mine hoax; Lewis reports him mentally unbalanced to spare him execution
