June 4 1944

A brutal act of vigilante revenge against Moroccan soldiers accused of atrocities is reported, carried out with ritualized, disturbing cruelty.

Themes

  • War & violence
  • Crime & banditry

Reflections

Selective reporting of Allied troops' crimes

After describing the vigilante killing of five Moroccan soldiers in reprisal for earlier atrocities, Lewis wonders drily whether any account of the episode will make it into the Allied Psychological Warfare Bureau's bulletin.
