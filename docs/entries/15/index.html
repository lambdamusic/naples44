October 6 1943

Arriving in a ruined, waterless Naples, the section settles into a grand requisitioned palazzo on the Riviera di Chiaia.

Themes

  • War & violence
  • Naples's character

Reflections

The psychology of informants

Lewis compares an informant to a duckling imprinting on the first sympathetic listener: driven by a strange, exclusive loyalty to one master rather than by payment, and never transferring allegiance once given.
