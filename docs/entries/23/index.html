October 23 1943

A false tip about mass delayed-action mines triggers a chaotic mass evacuation of Naples, including hospital patients and the bedridden.

Themes

  • War & violence
  • Bureaucracy & absurdity

Reflections

Naples seen from above — un-European, oriental

Awaiting a rumoured mass mine detonation from the heights of the Vomero, Lewis is struck, seeing the city whole for the first time, by how un-European and almost oriental Naples appears from a distance.
