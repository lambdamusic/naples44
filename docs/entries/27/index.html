November 1 1943

Lewis is indignant at the drastic cut to informant funds, arguing money isn't really what motivates their most dedicated Italian contacts.

Themes

  • Bureaucracy & absurdity
  • Personal reflection

Reflections

Conversion to pessimism — the blind orphan girls

Watching a group of weeping blind orphan girls ignored by indifferent diners in a restaurant, Lewis says the experience changed his outlook permanently: he had believed people eventually come to terms with pain, and now understands he was wrong.

Places

  • Vico Chiatamone Street
  • Vomero (Naples) District

People

  • Vincente Lattarullo Informant / contact reports on black-market corruption; Lewis witnesses his desperate poverty and the blind orphan girls scene with him
  • Sergeant-Major (John) Dashwood Allied colleague brings news of reduced informant funds

Saints & folklore

  • Hunchbacks as luck-bringers hunchbacks are considered lucky in Naples; lottery-ticket buyers touch or stroke a hunchback's hump as part of the purchase
