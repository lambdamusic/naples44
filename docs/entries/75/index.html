May 31 1944

Post-Fascist Italy's political life fragments into dozens of parties, some espousing bizarre or fringe ideas, reflecting a climate of instability.

Themes

  • Bureaucracy & absurdity
  • Personal reflection

Reflections

Postwar political fragmentation and 'respectable insanity'

Surveying Italy's sudden explosion of some sixty political parties, some proposing perpetual-motion machines as national policy, Lewis remarks that this is a season and situation in which insanity has become almost respectable.
