Entry 20 · Book chapter 21

October 15 1943

Introduction of Vincente Lattarullo, an eccentric, impoverished informant obsessed with justice and etiquette, who becomes a key civilian contact.

Themes

  • Poverty & hunger
  • Naples's character

Reflections

Educated poverty and family pride

Reflecting on Lattarullo, Lewis notes Naples has thousands of unemployed lawyers and doctors — families starve themselves so a son can be addressed with the honorific avvocato or dottore, even if he never practises.

People

  • Vincente Lattarullo Informant / contact starving non-practising Naples lawyer, Lewis's prize civilian contact; moved by a 'passion for justice'

Saints & folklore

  • Zio di Roma hired-mourner funeral custom — a well-dressed stranger poses as a dignified 'uncle from Rome' to add prestige to a family's funeral display
