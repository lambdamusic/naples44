December 9 1943

A Sunday spent observing the palazzo neighborhood's blend of aristocratic grandeur and thriving working-class street life.

Themes

  • Naples's character

Reflections

Naples's unconscious class proximity

Lewis observes that in his neighbourhood the rich and poor live physically side by side — grand palazzi and one-room working-class households sharing the same buildings — while remaining almost entirely unconscious of one another.
