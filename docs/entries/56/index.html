Entry 56 · Book chapter 57

March 25 1944

Widespread fear that San Gennaro's blood may fail to liquefy reflects a surge of religious "miracle" fervor sweeping wartime Naples.

Themes

  • Superstition & religion

Reflections

Miracle fervor as collective coping with crisis

Lewis observes that wartime hardship has pushed Naples back into the Middle Ages, with a sudden explosion of talking, bleeding, and weeping religious images answering a citywide craving for miracles and cures.

Saints & folklore

  • San Gennaro blood-liquefaction miracle, fear of its failure
  • Santo Agnello speaking crucifix
  • Santa Maria d'Intercessione
  • Santa Maria del Carmine image said to bow its head
  • St John / San Giovanni a Carbonara bubbling blood relic
  • Sant'Aspreno patron saint invoked against headaches; sufferers push their heads through a hole in the wall of his chapel seeking relief
