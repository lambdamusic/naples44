April 5 1944

Lewis describes the grim routine of vetting prospective war brides, most of whom turn out to be supporting themselves through prostitution out of dire poverty.

Themes

  • Poverty & hunger
  • Sex & relationships

Reflections

Economic desperation behind 'prostitution'

Investigating marriage candidates, Lewis finds most so-called prostitutes are ordinary girls with no other means of survival, and reflects that with the whole population out of work and producing nothing, it's remarkable they can even find a man to support them at all.
