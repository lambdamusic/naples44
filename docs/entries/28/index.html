Entry 28 · Book chapter 29

November 5 1943

New contacts Losurdo and Mosca reveal the same pattern as Lattarullo: impoverished nobility clinging to grand, half-empty palazzi.

Themes

  • Poverty & hunger
  • Naples's character

Reflections

Genteel poverty as a historical constant

Comparing his near-destitute contacts Lattarullo, Losurdo and Mosca to Alexandre Dumas's 1835 account of Naples's aristocracy, Lewis concludes their poverty isn't just a wartime effect but a century-old pattern of the city's oversupplied professional classes.
