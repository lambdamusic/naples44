August 21 1944

A schoolmaster explains the long, entrenched history of banditry in Southern Italy, including bandits' traditional ties to landowners.

Themes

  • Crime & banditry

Reflections

Fascism remembered as a 'golden interlude'

Discussing Southern Italy's long history of banditry with the schoolmaster Don Ubaldo, Lewis records that most people were already beginning to see the Fascist era as a golden interlude of security and firm government.
